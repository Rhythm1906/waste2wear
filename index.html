How it works

A traceable, simple process — from waste to wear.

Collect & Sort

We source high-quality post-consumer plastics and prepare them for extrusion.

Extrude Filament

Material is cleaned, melted, and extruded into consistent filament for printing.

Print & Finish

Printed parts are polished, assembled, and quality-checked by hand.
